# PRO content terms

## What PRO content is

Templates, premium effects and transitions, sound-library items, AI workflows (their steps, prompt
presets and references) and reference packs that we deliver to PRO accounts — through the MCP server
at mcp.greenlightdash.pro, inside GreenLight Dash, or as files an agent writes into your project.

PRO content is **not** part of the GreenLight Motion Engine and is **not** covered by its Apache License. (The
effects and transitions built into the engine are part of it, including the ones GreenLight Dash lists as PRO.)

## What you may do

- Use PRO content to make your own Content — videos, images, animations — including for commercial
  use and for clients, in any number of projects, while your PRO access is active.
- Keep, publish and sell the Content you made, forever, also after your PRO access ends.
- Change PRO content for your own projects.

## What you may not do

- Share, publish, sell or give the PRO **files** (or their contents) to anyone else — on their own,
  in a project folder you hand over, in a template pack, store or library, or inside a product.
  A client receives your finished Content, not the template; if a client needs the editable project,
  they need their own PRO access.
- Use PRO content to build templates, effects, sounds or workflows for others, or to train models that
  do.
- Remove or bypass the signatures, account marks or limits that come with PRO content.
- Use sound-library items outside Content made with the Software (for example as standalone music
  or in a sound pack).

## How it is delivered

- Per run, to the account that requested it, with that account's marks and signature.
- Within the usage limits shown in the app and on the site (for example, a cap on how many different
  workflows start per day).
- We may update or retire PRO content; Content you already made is not affected.

## Ending

If you break these terms, we may end your PRO access after notice. Content you already made stays
yours.
